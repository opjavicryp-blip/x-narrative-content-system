# Market Research Skill

## Objective

Systematically gather, organize, and synthesize market intelligence for analytical content creation.

## Research Framework

### Primary Sources

1. **Financial Data APIs**
   - ETF flows (daily/weekly)
   - Institutional holdings (13F filings)
   - Insider transactions (Form 4)
   - Earnings transcripts and estimates

2. **Macro Data**
   - Federal Reserve statements and dot plot
   - Inflation reports (CPI, PCE)
   - Employment data (NFP, unemployment rate)
   - Yield curve and liquidity metrics

3. **On-Chain Analytics** (for crypto)
   - Exchange flows
   - Miner/whale movements
   - Derivatives positioning (funding rates, OI)

### Secondary Sources

- Analyst reports (Bloomberg, PitchBook, NEJM, Cashmere)
- Industry newsletters and Substack
- X threads from credible analysts
- Reddit discussions (r/finance, r/investing, r/CryptoCurrency)
- YouTube analyst commentary

## Workflow

### Step 1: Define Research Question

Be specific:
- ❌ "What's happening with Bitcoin?"
- ✅ "What's driving BTC price action this week: ETF flows, macro, or technicals?"

### Step 2: Gather Data (30-45 min)

Use structured queries:
- ETF flow data (IBIT, FBTC, GBTC)
- Institutional positioning
- Macro catalysts (Fed speakers, CPI, NFP)
- Technical levels (key support/resistance)

### Step 3: Triangulate Narratives

Identify 2-3 competing explanations:
- Narrative A: ETF inflows driving price
- Narrative B: Macro liquidity improving
- Narrative C: Technical breakout attracting momentum

### Step 4: Fact-Check

Cross-reference claims:
- Verify ETF flow numbers across 2+ sources
- Check timestamps on social media claims
- Validate chart screenshots against original data

### Step 5: Synthesize

Create structured output:
- Key data points (3-5 bullets)
- Competing narratives (2-3 theories)
- Your contrarian take (1-2 sentences)
- Supporting evidence (links, charts)

## Tools & Connectors

| Tool | Use Case |
|------|----------|
| Finance API | ETF flows, institutional holdings, earnings |
| PitchBook/Cashmere | Private market data, analyst research |
| YouTube Data API | Find analyst commentary videos |
| Notion MCP | Store research in structured databases |
| GitHub | Version control for research notes |

## Output Format

```
## Research: [Topic]
## Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]

### Key Data
- [Data point 1 with source]
- [Data point 2 with source]

### Narratives
1. [Narrative A] - [Evidence]
2. [Narrative B] - [Evidence]

### Contrarian Take
[Your unique insight in 1-2 sentences]

### Sources
- [Link 1]
- [Link 2]
```

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] All numbers verified against primary sources
- [ ] Timestamps checked (no outdated data)
- [ ] At least 2 competing narratives identified
- [ ] Contrarian take is specific and falsifiable
- [ ] Sources are citable and accessible

## Common Mistakes

- Relying on single source for claims
- Using outdated data without noting timestamp
- Confusing correlation with causation
- Ignoring contradictory evidence
- Overcomplicating narrative (keep it simple)
