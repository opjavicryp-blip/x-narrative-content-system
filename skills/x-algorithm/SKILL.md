# X Algorithm Skill

## Objective

Understand and leverage X's ranking algorithm to maximize content distribution and engagement.

## Key Concepts

### Ranking Signals

1. **Recency** - Fresh content gets initial boost
2. **Engagement velocity** - Rapid early engagement signals quality
3. **Author authority** - Your historical performance on similar topics
4. **Media richness** - Posts with images/videos get 2-3x more reach
5. **Conversation depth** - Replies and threads increase dwell time

### Distribution Mechanics

- **First 30 minutes** critical for algorithmic testing
- **Engagement rate** (replies + reposts + likes) / impressions determines viral potential
- **Topic clustering** - X groups you by content themes; consistency matters
- **Network effects** - Engagement from high-authority accounts amplifies reach

## Workflow

### Pre-Publish

1. Check optimal posting time (8-10 AM, 6-9 PM local)
2. Prepare visual asset (chart, screenshot, or diagram)
3. Draft hook in first 120 characters
4. Include 1-2 relevant hashtags (not more)

### Post-Publish (First Hour)

1. Monitor engagement velocity
2. Reply to early comments within 15 minutes
3. If underperforming after 45 min, consider repost with variation
4. Track which topics resonate for future content

## Metrics to Track

| Metric | Target | Tool |
|--------|--------|------|
| Impression-to-engagement rate | >3% | X Analytics |
| Reply rate | >0.5% | X Analytics |
| Repost rate | >0.3% | X Analytics |
| Profile clicks per post | >1% | X Analytics |

## Common Mistakes

- Posting without visual media
- Weak or generic hooks
- Inconsistent topic focus
- Ignoring replies in first hour
- Overusing hashtags (>3 looks spammy)
