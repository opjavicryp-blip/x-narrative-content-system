# X Writing Skill

## Objective

Write clear, engaging, and actionable analytical posts optimized for X's format and audience.

## X Post Anatomy

```
[HOOK: First 120 characters - determines scroll-stop]

[BODY: 1-3 sentences with data or insight]

[VISUAL: Chart, screenshot, or diagram]

[CTA or punchline: Optional closer]
```

## Hook Formulas

### Data Hook
"Bitcoin ETFs just had their biggest outflow day in 3 months: -$412M yesterday. Here's what's happening..."

### Contrarian Hook
"Everyone's watching ETF flows. They're looking at the wrong metric."

### Question Hook
"Why did Bitcoin drop 8% on Wednesday? The answer isn't what you think."

### Statement Hook
"The Fed isn't the driver anymore. Liquidity is."

## Body Structure

### The Pyramid (Top-Down)

1. **Main point** (your thesis in 1 sentence)
2. **Supporting evidence** (2-3 data points)
3. **Implication** (so what? what's next?)

**Example:**
```
Bitcoin's rally is driven by macro, not ETFs.

• M2 growth turned positive last month
• Real yields peaked in July
• DXY is down 6% from highs

ETF flows are a lagging indicator. Liquidity leads.
```

### The Thread Structure

For longer analysis (3-10 tweets):

```
Tweet 1: Hook + main thesis
Tweet 2-4: Evidence (data, charts)
Tweet 5-7: Analysis (what it means)
Tweet 8: Conclusion + CTA
```

## Writing Principles

### Clarity Over Cleverness

❌ "The confluence of tailwinds portends bullish continuation"
✅ "Bitcoin is going up because of three things: ETF inflows, Fed pivot, and technical breakout"

### Specific Over Vague

❌ "Institutional demand is strong"
✅ "BlackRock's IBIT added $180M in BTC yesterday, the biggest inflow in 2 weeks"

### Active Over Passive

❌ "It is believed by analysts that..."
✅ "Analysts think..."

### Short Over Long

- Sentences: 10-20 words average
- Paragraphs: 1-2 sentences max
- Posts: 180-260 characters (leaves room for engagement)

## Tone Guidelines

### Confident, Not Arrogant

❌ "I called the top exactly. Follow me for more alpha."
✅ "This played out as expected. Here's what I'm watching next."

### Analytical, Not Emotional

❌ "This is insane! BTC is going to the moon!"
✅ "BTC up 7% today on $2B volume. Biggest move since August."

### Humble When Wrong

❌ [Delete the wrong post, pretend it never happened]
✅ "This didn't play out as I expected. Here's what I missed..."

## Visual Best Practices

### Charts

- Clear title and axis labels
- Highlight the relevant data point
- Source visible or cited
- Not cluttered (1-2 key lines/indicators)

### Screenshots

- Crop to relevant section
- Add arrow or box highlighting key number
- Include timestamp if time-sensitive

### Text Graphics

- Large, readable font
- High contrast (dark text on light background)
- 1 key point per image

## Engagement Optimization

### Reply Bait (Good Kind)

End with:
- "What am I missing?"
- "Curious if others see this differently"
- "Which factor matters most here?"

### Thread Timing

- Post thread all at once (not spaced out)
- First 3 tweets must stand alone (many won't read full thread)
- Number tweets (1/7, 2/7, etc.) for clarity

### Hashtags

- Use 1-2 max
- Relevant to topic (#Bitcoin, #ETF, #Fed)
- Not spammy (#crypto #trading #investing #money #wealth)

## Common Writing Mistakes

- **Weak hook**: First sentence doesn't grab attention
- **Too much context**: Assume audience knows basics
- **No visual**: Text-only posts get 2-3x less reach
- **Jargon**: "quantitative tightening" → "Fed reducing bond holdings"
- **No point**: Every post should have 1 clear takeaway
- **Overexplaining**: Trust audience to connect dots

## Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] Hook is specific and scroll-stopping
- [ ] Main point is clear in first 2 sentences
- [ ] Data is verified and sourced
- [ ] Visual adds value (not decorative)
- [ ] Post is under 280 characters (or thread is numbered)
- [ ] Tone is analytical, not emotional
- [ ] No jargon without explanation
- [ ] Spell-checked and grammar-checked

## Post Types

### Data Drop
Share 1-3 key numbers with context:
"GBTC outflows slowed to -$50M/day (from -$200M last week). Rotation into IBIT/FBTC continuing."

### Narrative Analysis
Explain competing explanations:
"Two views on this rally: (1) ETF flows, (2) macro liquidity. Here's the data for each..."

### Contrarian Take
Challenge consensus:
"Everyone's focused on ETFs. The real driver is [X]..."

### Catalyst Watch
Preview upcoming events:
"Watching 3 catalysts this week: CPI Wednesday, Fed speaker Thursday, NFP Friday. Here's what to expect..."

### Post-Mortem
Analyze what happened:
"BTC dropped 8% yesterday. Here are the 3 drivers..."

## Voice Development

Your voice = consistent patterns in:
- **Topic selection** (what you write about)
- **Framing** (how you interpret events)
- **Tone** (analytical, contrarian, educational)
- **Cadence** (how often, what format)
