# Writing Evals

## Purpose

Evaluate the quality of X posts and threads.

## Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Hook Strength | 25% | First 120 chars are scroll-stopping |
| Clarity | 20% | Main point clear in first 2 sentences |
| Evidence | 20% | Data-backed, sourced claims |
| Engagement Optimization | 15% | Visual, CTA, reply bait |
| Style Consistency | 10% | Matches voice/tone guidelines |
| Brevity | 10% | No fluff, every word earns its place |

## Scoring

- **A (90-100)**: Publish-ready, high engagement potential
- **B (80-89)**: Minor edits needed, good potential
- **C (70-79)**: Noticeable issues, revise before publishing
- **D (60-69)**: Significant rework needed
- **F (<60)**: Do not publish, rewrite from scratch

## Example Eval

**Post:** "Bitcoin ETFs had -$412M outflows yesterday. Biggest outflow day in 3 months..."

**Score:** A (94/100)

**Breakdown:**
- Hook Strength: 24/25 (specific, surprising number)
- Clarity: 20/20 (main point immediately clear)
- Evidence: 19/20 (data-backed, could add source link)
- Engagement Optimization: 15/15 (chart included, good CTA)
- Style Consistency: 10/10 (analytical, not emotional)
- Brevity: 6/10 (slightly long, could trim 20 chars)

**Feedback:**
- Strong hook with specific data
- Consider trimming last sentence
- Add source link in reply for credibility
