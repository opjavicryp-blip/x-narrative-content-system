# Eval: Visual Provenance Error (2026-09-06)

## Error

A chart was attributed to a specific commentator based solely on a matching search result and similar wording.

## Why It Failed

- The image itself identified only a data provider (e.g., Bloomberg) on the chart.
- It did not establish:
  - chart authorship;
  - publisher;
  - full methodology;
  - complete claim context;
  - observation or publication date.
- Matching wording in a search result is not proof of shared provenance.

## Correct Process

- Treat the image as a lead, not as proof of authorship.
- Verify separately:
  - original chart URL or document;
  - publication and timestamp;
  - creator/publisher;
  - series definition and methodology;
  - whether the chart headline matches the underlying data.
- Only after this verification assign authorship or use the chart as evidence for a specific claim.

## Lesson

Visual similarity and matching phrases do not establish provenance. Always trace charts and screenshots back to their original source before using them as evidence.
