# Negative Verification Case - Satoshi-Era Wallet Claim

## Trigger

A claim stated that several March 2010 wallets woke up and moved 50 BTC each, described as block rewards from the Satoshi era.

## Why It Was Blocked

A 50 BTC transfer is consistent with the original Bitcoin block subsidy. It does not establish:

- The originating UTXO was created in March 2010
- The funds were a coinbase output
- The wallet belonged to Satoshi Nakamoto
- The coins were sent to an exchange
- The movement was a sale
- The movement had a bearish market implication

## Required Evidence Before Publication

1. Transaction ID for each transfer
2. Explorer link
3. Block height and timestamp of the original UTXO
4. Coinbase ancestry confirmation
5. Destination-address analysis, if discussing intent
6. Independent source with methodology, where available

## Publication Status

```text
Status: BLOCKED
Reason: No transaction IDs, UTXO ancestry, or destination evidence.
```

## Publication-Safe Language After Verification

> "A few 50 BTC outputs mined in March 2010 moved today. The transfers appear to trace back to early coinbase rewards. A move is not automatically a sale."

Use only after the on-chain evidence establishes the claimed provenance.

## Lesson

Do not confuse amount, age, provenance, ownership, destination, and intent. They are separate claims requiring separate evidence.
