# Research Evals

## Purpose

Evaluate the quality of market research outputs.

## Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Source Quality | 30% | % of claims backed by Tier 1 or Tier 2 sources |
| Data Accuracy | 25% | All numbers verified against primary sources |
| Narrative Coverage | 20% | At least 2 competing narratives identified |
| Contrarian Insight | 15% | Unique, falsifiable thesis presented |
| Clarity | 10% | Structured, easy to follow |

## Scoring

- **A (90-100)**: All criteria met at high level
- **B (80-89)**: Minor gaps, overall strong
- **C (70-79)**: Noticeable gaps, needs improvement
- **D (60-69)**: Significant issues, rework needed
- **F (<60)**: Unacceptable quality

## Example Eval

**Research Topic:** Bitcoin ETF flows driving price

**Score:** B+ (87/100)

**Breakdown:**
- Source Quality: 28/30 (all Tier 1-2 sources)
- Data Accuracy: 25/25 (all numbers verified)
- Narrative Coverage: 18/20 (2 narratives, could add 3rd)
- Contrarian Insight: 12/15 (good take, not fully developed)
- Clarity: 9/10 (well structured)

**Feedback:**
- Add 3rd competing narrative (macro liquidity)
- Develop contrarian thesis with more evidence
- Otherwise strong research
