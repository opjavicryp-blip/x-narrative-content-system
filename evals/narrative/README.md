# Narrative Evals

## Purpose

Evaluate the quality of narrative analysis outputs.

## Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Narrative Identification | 25% | All major competing narratives captured |
| Evidence Quality | 25% | Each narrative backed by data/sources |
| Momentum Assessment | 20% | Clear view on which narratives gaining/losing |
| Contrarian Analysis | 20% | Insightful alternative perspective |
| Inflection Awareness | 10% | Catalysts that could flip narratives identified |

## Scoring

- **A (90-100)**: All criteria met at high level
- **B (80-89)**: Minor gaps, overall strong
- **C (70-79)**: Noticeable gaps, needs improvement
- **D (60-69)**: Significant issues, rework needed
- **F (<60)**: Unacceptable quality

## Example Eval

**Topic:** Bitcoin rally narratives (September 2026)

**Score:** A- (92/100)

**Breakdown:**
- Narrative Identification: 24/25 (4 narratives captured)
- Evidence Quality: 24/25 (all backed by data)
- Momentum Assessment: 18/20 (clear on gaining/losing)
- Contrarian Analysis: 18/20 (strong macro liquidity take)
- Inflection Awareness: 8/10 (could add more catalysts)

**Feedback:**
- Excellent narrative mapping
- Add 2-3 more specific inflection triggers
- Consider tracking narrative mentions over time
