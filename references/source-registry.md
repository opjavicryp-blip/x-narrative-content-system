## Sources to Avoid (Tier 4)

- Anonymous X accounts without source links
- Reddit/Telegram rumors without verification
- Old articles presented as current news
- Screenshots without context or timestamps
- "Sources say" without naming sources

## Verification Protocol

When using any source:

1. **Check timestamp**: Is this current or old data?
2. **Check methodology**: How was this calculated?
3. **Cross-reference**: Can I verify with 2+ sources?
4. **Assess credibility**: What's their track record?
5. **Document**: Record source URL and verification notes
