# Evidence Policy Reference

## Purpose

Define what counts as evidence, how it must be checked, and what can be published.

## Evidence Hierarchy

### Tier 1 - Primary evidence

- Original filing, official company release, prospectus, earnings transcript
- Central bank, Treasury, BLS, BEA, FRED, SEC, CFTC, exchange, issuer, or fund-administrator data
- Direct blockchain transaction plus block height, UTXO ancestry, and explorer link

### Tier 2 - Reputable reporting

Reporting that directly cites and preferably links to Tier 1 evidence.

### Tier 3 - Specialist research

Data providers, analysts, newsletters, and dashboards with disclosed methodology. Use as corroboration or leads when Tier 1 is unavailable.

### Tier 4 - Leads only

Screenshots, cropped charts, social posts, anonymous accounts, unattributed graphics, and second-hand summaries.

## Rules

1. A screenshot is a lead, not evidence.
2. A chart is not evidence without source, date, units, cohort definition, and methodology.
3. Quotes require exact wording and a direct source. Paraphrases do not use quotation marks.
4. Current facts must be refreshed immediately before drafting.
5. Historical/contextual research is unrestricted but must be labelled CONTEXT.
6. Calculations must show inputs, formula, signed values, and time interval.
7. A 50 BTC transfer does not prove a 2010 coinbase origin, Satoshi ownership, or sale intent.
8. A fund flow does not prove spot buying or selling at that moment.
9. A job posting proves hiring, not product launch or client adoption.
10. "Building", "offering", "recommending exposure", "custody", and "client adoption" are separate claims.
11. Do not infer causation from timing alone.
12. If a primary source cannot be found, attribute the claim, state the limitation, and lower confidence.
13. When sources conflict, do not average them into a fact. Explain the disagreement or block the claim.
14. A visually plausible infographic is not self-authenticating.

## Evidence Labels

### CURRENT
Information relevant now. Default research window: last 24 hours. Include a timestamp.

### CONTEXT
Historical, structural, or background information. No 24-hour restriction. Include date or period.

### NARRATIVE
Prior account posts, theses, and lessons. Use for continuity only, not to establish external facts.

## Minimum Publish Standard

A publication-bound factual claim needs:
- Exact claim wording
- Evidence source and tier
- Data timestamp
- Claim scope
- Calculation check where relevant
- Contradiction check
- Status: Verified or safely qualified as Partially Verified
